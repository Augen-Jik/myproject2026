\subsection{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

\paragraph{Compositional-OOD grounding frontier.}
STAG's grounding is invariant to graph scale, topology renaming, and linguistic paraphrase (all
$\sim$100\% offline), but degrades to 45--55\% under \emph{compositional} OOD---multiple simultaneous
conflicting events on a single instance (\S\ref{sec:p4reconcile}). The closed-loop $0.96$ is measured at
operationally realistic conflict density and should be read as such; the amplified multi-conflict tail is
an open capability gap, not a frame artifact.

\paragraph{Edge-/segment-level precision is open.}
Held-out transfer is established at the \emph{road} level (recall $0.93$); edge-exact and segment-level
(\texttt{range}) precision remain low (0.07--0.14) and are deferred to range-aware evaluation. We do not
claim segment-exact localization is solved. The R4 cross-topology appendix surfaces the same weak axis in
a bounded form: on $M5$ Irregular, directional accuracy is $82.0$ while road-level recall, schema
validity, temporal quality, conflict resolution, hallucination rate, and truncation remain stable; we
treat this as a topology-related offline grounding-quality wrinkle consistent with the P4 OOD-topology
split and the P5 direction-load-bearing ablation, not as a separate limitation or a closed-loop failure
claim.

\paragraph{Held-out closed-loop transfer: resolved on a discriminative set.}
Our first attempt to extend closed-loop evaluation to held-out SUMO networks was \emph{frame-blocked}:
on the initially frozen held-out maps over $90\%$ of ground-truth blockages collapsed onto a single
row/column index, so projecting a road-level prediction onto the materialized network was not faithfully
scorable (a perfect-predictor control confirmed the harness was otherwise fair). We resolved this by
constructing a \emph{discriminative} held-out set with faithful per-road edge geometry and verified
frame-position dispersion (M2\_GridM, M3\_GridL; \S\ref{sec:robustness}), on which STAG transfers
($119/120$) while the dense interface collapses ($0/120$). We retain the methodological observation that
constructing discriminative held-out closed-loop benchmarks is nontrivial and, to our knowledge,
underprovided by current synthetic generators. What remains open is \emph{edge-/segment-exact} precision,
not closed-loop transfer itself.

\paragraph{Phrasing brittleness (narrow, input-normalizable).}
Stress-testing instruction phrasing surfaced one narrow brittleness: an out-of-distribution paraphrase
that inserts a comma between the range and direction clauses of a single moderate-congestion
(\texttt{simple\_local}) event can trigger a \texttt{NO\_ANCHOR} fallback. The benchmark distribution does
\emph{not} contain this construction---canonical \texttt{simple\_local} concatenates the clauses without a
separator---and the contrastive comma construction in \texttt{directional\_asymmetry} events is
unaffected, so main-table results are not impacted; it is resolvable by input normalization without
retraining.

\paragraph{Backbone correctness is family-dependent.}
The typed-anchor \emph{interface} generates robustly across Qwen2.5 and Llama-3.2, but absolute
correctness is Qwen-favored and partly confounded by chat-template/EOS and output-length differences. We
claim interface robustness, not equal cross-family correctness, and make no scale-necessity claim.

\paragraph{Baseline and efficiency caveats.}
The DynamicRouteGPT comparison uses a backbone-matched substitution (Qwen2.5-1.5B, not the original
Llama3-8B) and is a fine-tuned-vs-zero-shot contrast (footnotes a--c). DCRNN is numeric/NL-blind and given
a near-oracle sensor; its 0.80 must be read by bucket. DQN is a learned-control baseline given the same
oracle-grade sensor: its reported constraint satisfaction is completion-conditional and sensing-fidelity
(effective $\mathrm{CR}\times\mathrm{CS}=0.66$), and it collapses on the compositional buckets (footnote~f).
STAG accepts a travel-time trade-off (worst Time Loss) to satisfy constraints; the CS-conditional
comparison is unpaired. Graph WaveNet and LLM-A$^\star$ are reported as not-run-with-reason.
